Running

Almgren wins New York in his marathon debut

The Swede ran 2:06:01, breaking away from Yeman Crippa late. He picked New York because position matters more than the clock there. Chepkirui regained the women's title.

“I didn't really look at my watch or the time or try to calculate a finishing time or anything. I just tried to stay up in the front then see what happened.” - Andreas Almgren, at the post-race press conference
“It would have been nice to have a 2:05 time, but I'm very happy about the win.” - Almgren

San Francisco

SF got $7M to expand its innovation team. Lurie just fired most of it

Six of 10 staffers were fired Friday with no severance. City Hall won't say whether it met the conditions on Bloomberg's largest-ever city innovation grant, including a $2.38M payment due Nov. 1.

“No positions have been eliminated.” - Mayor's office statement, which did not dispute that six of 10 staffers lost their jobs
“With that technology becoming more capable than it was even a couple of years ago, the Mayor's Office of Innovation should be capable of tackling a wide range of challenges.” - Charles Lutvak, Lurie spokesperson

San Francisco

Lurie gains power - and loses excuses

Props D, E and F passed with roughly $14M in billionaire backing, handing the mayor more control over ballots, contracts and department heads. Analysts say the blame game ends here.

“These charter reform measures will make it very hard now for mayors to argue credibly that they don't have the authority to do what voters ask of them.” - Jason McDaniel, SFSU political science professor
“He has all the power he asked for. Let's see what he does with it.” - Eric Jaye, Democratic political consultant
“These victories are not a finish line, they are a starting point.” - Daniel Lurie, in his victory speech Tuesday night

San Francisco

Bay Area voters pull transit back from the brink

The Regional Transit Measure, a sales tax worth roughly $1B a year for BART, Muni, Caltrain and AC Transit, won 62% region-wide and 79% in SF. Prop H's Muni parcel tax took 74%.

“This is existential, especially for BART.” - Tom Radulovich, former BART board member, before the vote
