San Francisco

SF voters pass Props D, E and F after $14M billionaire-backed campaign, handing Lurie sole power over department heads

Props D, E and F passed with roughly $14M in billionaire backing, handing the mayor more control over ballots, contracts and department heads. Analysts say the blame game ends here.

Regional Transit Measure passes with 62% region-wide and 79% in SF; ~$1B/yr sales tax saves BART, Muni, Caltrain from 2027 cuts
